# Giving to Others

### Opening Prayer
Take, Lord, and receive all
my liberty,
my memory, my
understanding,
and my entire will,
All I have and call my own.
You have given all to me.
To you, Lord, I return it.
Everything is yours; do
with it what you will.
Give me only your love and
your grace,
that is enough for me. *-St Ignatius of Loyola*

### Activity
- *Pass around cards for each person to write how they will give of themselves*
- No one will share these, but take them with you as a reminder and a promise to oneself

### Closing Prayer: Prayer for Genorosity

Lord, teach me to be generous.  
Teach me to serve you as you deserve; 
To give and not to count the cost; 
To fight and not to heed the wounds; 
To toil and not to seek for rest; 
To labor and not to ask for reward, 
Save that of knowing that I do your will. *- Commonly attributed to St Ignatius, most likely inspired by his works and written in the early 1900's*
